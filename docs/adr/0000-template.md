# NNNN — Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-slug.md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play: technical constraints, thesis requirements, time budget, team
size. State the problem neutrally; do not argue for the decision here.

## Decision

The choice made, in one or two sentences, followed by the reasoning. Name the
alternatives that were considered and why each was rejected.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work the decision creates.
Include negative consequences honestly; examiners value trade-off awareness.
