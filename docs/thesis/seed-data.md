# Synthetic dataset: design and assumptions

The evaluation dataset is generated, not collected. This note records how, so that the
thesis can state precisely what the models were trained and evaluated on, and so that the
dataset can be regenerated bit-for-bit by anyone with the repository.

| Component                    | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Licence / status                       |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |

| Points of interest           | OpenStreetMap via Overpass. | Buildings and listings       | Generated by `apps/api/prisma/seed.ts`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Synthetic                              |
| Exchange rates               | 30-day random walk around approximate 2026 CBA reference rates (USD 385.5, EUR 447)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Synthetic                              |
| District market history      | 12 monthly snapshots per district derived from the calibrated medians                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Synthetic                              |

No listing portal or classifieds site is accessed. OSM data is downloaded once by
`pnpm --filter @smartestate/api osm:fetch`, committed as GeoJSON, and the seed runs offline.

## Reproducibility

- RNG: mulberry32 with seed `20260911`; reference date `2026-09-01T00:00:00Z`.
- Running `pnpm db:seed` twice yields identical listings, prices, texts and dates.
- Changing the seed constant in `prisma/seed/data/calibration.ts` produces a different but
  equally reproducible dataset for robustness checks.

## Price model

For each listing, price per m² is

```
median(district) × m_type(buildingType) × m_cond(condition) × m_floor(floor, totalFloors, elevator) × m_ceiling(height) × exp(N(0, σ_district))
```

with the multipliers in `prisma/seed/lib/pricing.ts`:

| Building type | m_type | Condition       | m_cond |
| ------------- | -----: | --------------- | -----: |
| New build     |   1.20 | Designer        |   1.25 |
| Monolith      |   1.15 | Euro renovation |   1.12 |
| Stalinka      |   1.05 | Good            |   1.00 |
| Stone         |   1.00 | Old renovation  |   0.88 |
| Panel         |   0.85 | Needs repair    |   0.75 |
| Khrushchyovka |   0.78 |                 |        |

Floor: ground −6 %, top without lift −5 %, top with lift −2 %, floors 2–4 +3 %, floors ≥ 5
without lift −3 %. Ceiling: ≥ 3.2 m +5 %, ≥ 3.0 m +3 %, < 2.6 m −2 %.

About 55 % of listings are quoted in USD (rounded to 500 USD) and converted to canonical AMD
with the seeded rate; the rest are quoted in AMD rounded to 100 000. Around 30 % of listings
carry one earlier, 3–9 % higher price in their history to exercise price-drop features.

## Why a known structure matters

Because the generating process is explicit, the valuation model in Phase 7 can be checked
against ground truth: a correct model should recover building-type and condition effects close
to the table above and should not find effects that were not seeded. Spatial cross-validation
(holding out entire districts) remains meaningful because district medians differ.

## Limitations to state in the thesis

- Prices follow the assumed structure by construction; real markets contain effects the seed
  omits (view, street noise, developer reputation, negotiation outcomes).
- Building coordinates are uniform within district polygons, not clustered along streets.
- Listing photos are placeholders (`picsum.photos`), flagged `is_placeholder = true`.
- Rent estimates derive from a single assumed gross yield of 6.5 %.
